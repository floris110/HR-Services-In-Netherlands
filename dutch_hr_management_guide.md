# Managing Dutch HR When Your Company Is International

If you run an international company in the Netherlands, Dutch HR responsibilities can quickly become part of your daily workload. Hiring, payroll, employment contracts, compliance, and workforce support all involve local requirements. You may also need to manage sickness absence, prepare HR policies, or deal with a dismissal procedure while keeping your wider business moving.

The difficulty is not only knowing what needs to be done. You also need to apply Dutch employment law correctly and keep your HR processes consistent. That is where external HR support can fit into your organisation. Instead of building every process internally, you can use support for a specific task, an urgent issue, or a wider HR structure.

## Dutch HR Support from HRHelp Netherlands

### How These Areas Connect

These responsibilities rarely exist in isolation:

* An **employment contract** must reflect both the operational role and mandatory Dutch legal requirements.
* **HR policies** provide employees and managers with clear, day-to-day guidance.
* **Payroll management** must seamlessly integrate with HR records and contractual terms.
* A **compliance audit** identifies gaps across your operational processes before issues emerge.

## Choosing the Right Level of HR Support

Not every international company requires the same arrangement. Your team might need targeted advice for a single employment contract, guidance on the 30% Ruling, or hands-on payroll coordination. Alternatively, you might require a fully outsourced HR department overseeing every stage of the employee lifecycle.

| Priority Area / Pain Point | Recommended Service Focus |
|---|---|
| **Managerial time lost to employee absence** | Sickness Management |
| **Outdated guidelines or legal uncertainty** | HR Policies / Compliance Audit |
| **Scaling or restructuring the Dutch team** | HR Reset / HR Teams |
| **Market entry & foundational setup** | HR Settlers |
| **Time-critical workplace escalations** | HR S.O.S. |
