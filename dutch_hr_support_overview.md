# Dutch HR Support for International Companies

Running a team in the Netherlands means dealing with local HR requirements alongside core business operations. Hiring, payroll, employment contracts, sickness absence, and dismissal procedures all follow strict Dutch regulations that require careful navigation. The same applies when establishing HR processes for the first time or supporting a distributed workforce across multiple countries.

This complexity increases when internal management or People teams are based outside the Netherlands:

* **Employment Law Fit:** Policies standard in other jurisdictions often do not align with mandatory Dutch labor laws.
* **Payroll Alignment:** Payroll data must be consistently reconciled against localized contracts and statutory benefits.
* **Sickness Absence:** Sickness management requires a structured, compliant process with detailed documentation and statutory follow-up timelines under the Dutch Gatekeeper Improvement Act (*Wet verbetering poortwachter*).

## Compliance Audits in the Netherlands

If you are reviewing company operations, an HR compliance audit provides a structured framework to evaluate risk exposure and operational readiness:

* **Documentation & Contracts:** Reviewing employment contracts, addenda, and non-compete clauses against current Dutch labor statutes.
* **Payroll Practices:** Ensuring wage tax, collective labor agreement (CAO) obligations, and holiday allowances are managed accurately.
* **Internal Policies:** Checking handbooks, remote-work agreements, and privacy regulations (GDPR/AVG).
* **Restructuring Support:** Identifying procedural gaps before scaling, restructuring, or acquiring an entity.

An audit provides prioritized visibility into what requires immediate remediation versus long-term adjustment before issues escalate.

| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HR Settlers** | Foundation & Setup | Establishing compliant HR frameworks and first contracts in the Netherlands. |
| **HR Teams** | Retained / Outsourced HR | Ongoing operational management for day-to-day employee affairs. |
