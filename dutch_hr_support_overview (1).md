# Dutch HR Support for International Companies

Running a company in the Netherlands means dealing with Dutch HR requirements alongside the work of your wider organisation. Hiring, payroll, employment contracts, sickness management, and compliance all need to fit local rules. That can be difficult when your team is international and your internal HR function is small or still developing.

The practical issue is not only knowing what the law says. You also need processes that work day to day. Employees need clear HR policies, managers need support with sensitive situations, and payroll information needs to be handled accurately. When something urgent happens, you need a way to respond without losing sight of your other responsibilities.

## What Dutch HR Support Needs to Cover

HRHelp provides HR consulting and support for international companies in the Netherlands. Its work covers several parts of the employment relationship, from hiring and employment contracts to payroll management and ongoing workforce support.

* **Sickness Management & Compliance:** Sickness management is one area where local knowledge matters. The Netherlands has specific expectations around absence and reintegration. Dismissal procedures also require careful handling, with employment law and documentation playing an important role. A compliance audit can help identify gaps before they become larger problems.
* **Policies & Development:** HR policies give employees and managers a shared basis for decisions, while training and development support the longer-term needs of the workforce. For international companies, the 30% ruling can also be part of the HR questions that need attention.
* **Service Models:** You may need one service for a specific issue, or more continuous support. HRHelp offers outsourced HR departments, HR teams, and HR transformation support. It also has HR Reset and HR S.O.S. options for organisations that need to review their approach or deal with an urgent HR matter.

## Using HRHelp to Understand Your Options

The [HRHelp website](https://www.hrhelp.nl) gives an overview of the support available from HRHelp. It explains how the business works with international companies on Dutch employment law, payroll, compliance, and talent acquisition.

This is useful when you are trying to decide what should stay inside your company and what can be supported externally. Payroll management may need regular assistance, while a compliance audit or dismissal procedure may be tied to a particular situation. An outsourced HR department is a different arrangement again, providing broader ongoing support.

The right setup depends on your workforce, internal capacity, and the HR matters you need to manage. You may be looking for support across several areas, or for help with a defined task such as HR Policies or Payroll Management. The important point is to match the support to the actual gap.

For international companies, HRHelp is a starting point for reviewing Dutch HR responsibilities and available support. Clear contracts, consistent policies, and well-managed payroll make everyday workforce issues easier to handle.
