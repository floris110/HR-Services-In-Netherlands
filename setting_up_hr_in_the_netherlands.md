# Setting Up HR for Your First Netherlands Employee

Opening a company presence in the Netherlands brings practical HR work with it from the start. You need to understand Dutch employment requirements, prepare the right documents, and organise support for employees once hiring begins. These tasks can be difficult to separate from the wider setup of your business, especially when your existing HR processes were built for another country.

For an international company, the main challenge is often knowing what needs to happen first. Entity registration, employment contracts, payroll, compliance, and ongoing workforce support are connected. Missing one part can create extra work later, particularly when you are handling Dutch HR matters from outside the Netherlands.

## What to Prepare Before Hiring

Before hiring, clarify how the Dutch entity will be handled and which HR responsibilities need to be covered locally. Entity registration is part of the initial setup, while payroll, contracts, policies, and compliance continue after recruitment.

* **Contracts:** Must reflect the standard terms and legal requirements in the Netherlands.
* **Payroll:** Processes must be configured and ready to process employee payments on time.
* **Policies:** Provide clear, localized workplace guidelines.
* **Compliance Audits:** Help review whether ongoing processes remain aligned with local laws.

### Ongoing Workforce Support

The support model can extend beyond the initial setup. Ongoing workforce support includes:

* Sickness management
* Training and development
* Dismissal procedures
* Urgent HR support

HR responsibilities do not stop when the first contract is signed. For companies comparing options, the HR Settlers model refers specifically to the complete setup service rather than a single standalone HR task.
